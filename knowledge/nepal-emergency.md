# Nepal emergency guidance

**Status:** verified reference, reviewed 2026-10-03

## Emergency contacts

- Medical ambulance: **102** is the Nepal Ambulance Service / Ambulance Nepal
  number. Coverage and dispatch availability are not guaranteed nationwide;
  location and local provider coverage matter.
- Nepal Police control: **100**.
- Fire services: **101** is commonly listed for Kathmandu emergency activation;
  outside the valley, use the local fire or emergency service.
- Do not state that **112** is a universal nationwide Nepal emergency number.
  Older emergency-access guidance mentions it for mobile calls, but coverage
  and routing must be confirmed before presenting it as guaranteed.

## Operator instructions

- Use the caller's language when possible (English or Nepali).
- Never invent a facility capability, bed count, emergency number, or ETA.
- Clearly distinguish live feed data, verified static facts, and uncertainty.
- If a contact number may not work in the caller's area, say so and suggest the
  local emergency service or nearest hospital.
- For immediate danger, tell the caller to contact emergency services first;
  JeevanRoute is not a replacement for dispatch or clinical care.
